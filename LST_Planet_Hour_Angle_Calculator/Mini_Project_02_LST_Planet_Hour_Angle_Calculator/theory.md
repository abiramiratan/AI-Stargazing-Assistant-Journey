# Theory — LST & Planet Hour Angle Calculator

## Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time (GMST)

GMST is the Right Ascension currently crossing the
Greenwich meridian (0° longitude).

It serves as the reference sidereal time from which
Local Sidereal Time is calculated for any location
on Earth.

---

## Local Sidereal Time (LST)

LST is the Right Ascension currently crossing the
observer's local meridian.

It depends on the observer's longitude and indicates
which part of the celestial sphere is directly overhead
along the meridian at a given moment.

---

## Right Ascension (RA)

Right Ascension is the celestial equivalent of
longitude on Earth.

It specifies the east-west position of a celestial
object on the celestial sphere and is measured in
hours, minutes, and seconds.

Objects with larger RA values appear farther east
on the celestial sphere.

---

## Declination (Dec)

Declination is the celestial equivalent of latitude.

It specifies how far north or south a celestial object
is from the celestial equator and is measured in degrees.

* Positive Declination → North of the Celestial Equator
* Negative Declination → South of the Celestial Equator

---

## Hour Angle (HA)

Hour Angle tells how far an object has moved from the
observer's meridian.

It represents the difference between the observer's
Local Sidereal Time and the object's Right Ascension.

### Interpretation

**HA = 0**

* Object is crossing the local meridian
* Highest point in the sky for that day

**0 < HA < 12**

* Object is west of the meridian
* Object is moving toward setting

**12 < HA < 24**

* Object is east of the meridian
* Object is moving toward rising
